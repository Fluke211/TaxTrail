Coming to the App Store

Your receipts never leave your phone.

TaxTrail scans a receipt, reads it on your device, and files it against the right IRS Schedule C line. No account. No cloud. No ads.

Why on-device matters. Every other receipt app uploads your purchases to a server so software there can read them. TaxTrail uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs on the phone itself — so there is no server holding a record of where you shop, what you buy, or what you earn. Not because we promise not to look. Because there is nothing to look at.

What it does

  • Reads the receipt on your device. Edge detection and perspective correction first, then text recognition — the photo quality is what limits accuracy, so the scanner fixes that before reading.
  • Files it against Schedule C. 29 tax categories mapped to actual IRS lines, plus Schedule A, Form 8829, Form 4562, COGS and personal.
  • Handles sales tax properly. Separates the taxable base from the tax, remembers merchant and city rates, and gets the math right on receipts that print a rate instead of an amount.
  • Splits one receipt across categories. The hardware-store run that was half job materials and half personal, with the tax apportioned.
  • Exports for your CPA. CSV, Excel, TXF, and QuickBooks — plus a full archive with every receipt image, so you can throw the paper out.
  • Multi-page capture for the long ones that never fit in a single frame.
